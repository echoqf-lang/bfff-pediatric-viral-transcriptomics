
# Data provenance and redistribution boundary

## Transcriptomic inputs

All six expression datasets were obtained from the NCBI Gene Expression Omnibus. The completed study analyzed processed Series Matrix expression values and accompanying public metadata. It did not analyze raw CEL, IDAT, FASTQ, SRA, or equivalent primary archives. Downloaded expression matrices are not redistributed in this package; accession identifiers, sample manifests, acquisition records, and checksums are retained instead.

## Candidate-space inputs

The BFFF candidate space was defined from an institution-specific 14-herb prescription and database-derived compound–target records. The prescription definition was used only to construct the candidate search space. Full TCMSP and BATMAN-TCM exports are excluded because redistribution permission has not been established. The package retains compact frozen gene sets, mapping rules, provenance summaries, and analysis configuration required to interpret the reported results.

The institution's internal clinical document is not part of this archive and must not be uploaded to a public repository.

## Interpretation

The archived outputs describe disease-associated transcriptomic organization and candidate prioritization. They are not evidence of treatment exposure, pharmacological binding, target occupancy, efficacy, clinical benefit, or causal mechanism.

## Reproduction boundary

The archive preserves code, dependency declarations, processed-input identifiers, checksums, sample manifests, diagnostics, and derived results. A completely fresh end-to-end rerun from newly downloaded GEO files has not been performed as part of repository staging and is not claimed by `VALIDATION_REPORT.txt`.
